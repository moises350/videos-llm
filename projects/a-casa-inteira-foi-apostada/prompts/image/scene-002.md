# Scene 002 — imagem-chave

Same Brazil-2026 room and same fully human family as scene 001, now visibly
emptier. Grocery bags and a bank card are already absent; in the later state,
the construction helmet and framed family photographs are absent too. Preserve
the room, bodies, clothing, furniture, camera position and smartphone exactly
so the loss is read through the empty spaces. The family becomes more
desaturated and separated, never physically transformed. Wide locked broadcast
tableau, authentic mid-1990s Betacam SP capture, 4:3 source inside vertical
9:16, interlacing, chroma bleed, dropout, low dynamic range, flat practical
light, native low definition.

Negative: no luminous ribbons, no objects flying, no magic sparkles, no clean
CGI particles, no rat traits, no melting bodies, no readable betting logo, no
text, no luxury interior, no 4K detail, no horror gore.
