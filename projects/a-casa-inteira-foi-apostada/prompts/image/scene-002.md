# Scene 002 — imagem-chave

Same Brazil-2026 room and same fully human family as scene 001, now visibly
emptier. Grocery bags, a bank card, a construction work helmet and framed
family photographs unravel into physical luminous ribbons of unstable green
and magenta composite-video color that converge on the contemporary
smartphone. The sofa and table remain partially present so the loss is readable;
the family is desaturated and separated, not physically transformed. Wide
locked broadcast tableau, authentic mid-1990s Betacam SP capture, 4:3 source
inside vertical 9:16, interlacing, chroma bleed, dropout, low dynamic range,
flat practical light, native low definition.

Negative: no magic sparkles, no clean CGI particles, no rat traits, no melting
bodies, no readable betting logo, no text, no luxury interior, no 4K detail,
no horror gore.
